     §4. Analysis of an Abstract Variational Problem   
                                                       
     In this paragraph, we construct an abstract framework well adapted to the
     solution of a variety of linear boundary value problems with a constraint, like
     the Stokes problem. Several algorithms are proposed to deal with the constraint.
     Although they are introduced in connection with the continuous problem, they
     will prove to be useful mainly for solving the discretized problems.
