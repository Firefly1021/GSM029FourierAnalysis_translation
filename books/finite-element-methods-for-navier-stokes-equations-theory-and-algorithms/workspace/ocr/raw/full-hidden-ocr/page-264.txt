     Remark 4.4. The above theorem calls for a number of comments. First of all, it
     is obvious that this approach yields very neatly optimal error estimates for
     polynomials of all degrees. In addition, the scheme considered is fairly inexpensive.
       On the other hand, all results are stated for a right-hand side f in L?(Q)?
     whereas one is often interested in solving the Stokes problem when the right-hand
     side is in L’(Q)? withr < 2. The next section extends the error analysis to this case.
