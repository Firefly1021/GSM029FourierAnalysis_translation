     §4. Numerical Analysis of Centered Finite Element Schemes
                                                       
     In this paragraph, we propose to apply to the homogeneous Navier-Stokes
     equations most of the finite element methods developed in Chapters II and III
     to solve the Stokes problem. The reader will find that in nearly every case, the
     error analysis carries over successfully to Navier-Stokes equations. One of the
     few exceptions is the three-dimensional mixed method elaborated in Paragraph
     IfI.5. This method can be tried to solve the Navier-Stokes system but at the
     present stage, its numerical analysis is still an open problem.
