   A.3. Interpolation of Discontinuous Functions      
                                                      
   The interpolation of L' functions was first analyzed by Clément [21] who
   proposed a local regularization operator. This technique was later generalized by
   Bernardi [9] who introduced more general finite elements in order to interpolate
   essentially functions that were either discontinuous or that were defined on
   domains with curved boundaries. We have no space here to describe “curved”
   finite elements but we shall discuss briefly the interpolation of functions that
   are not supposed to be continuous. For the sake of simplicity, we shall restrict
   ourselves to the finite element space O, defined by (A.18) on triangular finite
   elements. The forthcoming results are valid for higher dimensions and quadrilat-
   eral finite elements and also for spaces with other boundary conditions, like for
