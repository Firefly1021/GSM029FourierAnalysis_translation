     §6. Numerical Algorithms                          
                                                       
     Navier-Stokes equations are difficult to solve in practice because they are non-
     linear. We present here a few simple converging algorithms that permit to handle
     the nonlinearity. Although they are intended to solve the discrete systems of
     (nonlinear) equations, it is simpler to introduce these algorithms in connection
     with the continuous problem. The reader will verify easily that the convergence
     theorems below are also valid for the approximate problems.
