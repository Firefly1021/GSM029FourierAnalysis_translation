      § 1. General Approximation                       
                                                       
      The abstract problem discussed in Chapter I, § 4 lends itself readily to a straight-
      forward approximation that converges under reasonable assumptions with an
      error proportional to the approximation error of the spaces involved. When
      applied to the Stokes problem, this approach yields a conforming approximation
      of the velocity and pressure, although the approximate velocity field is (in general)
      not exactly divergence-free. The wide range of finite element methods developped
      in the remainder of the chapter are all founded on the material of this paragraph.
      Non-conforming methods can also be put into this framework (cf. Zine [85]) but
      for the sake of conciseness we have skipped them entirely.
