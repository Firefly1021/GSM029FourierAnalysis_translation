   §1. Mixed Approximation of an Abstract Problem     
                                                      
                                                      
   In this paragraph, we concentrate again upon the abstract problem studied in
   Chapter I § 4, but we put it into a weaker setting leading to a (generally) different
   mixed formulation. The mixed approximation derived from this formulation will
   give rise to the important class of exactly incompressible methods to solve the
   Stokes and Navier-Stokes equations.
