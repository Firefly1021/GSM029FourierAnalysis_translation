    1.4. Checking the inf-sup Condition               
                                                      
    This short section is dedicated to the construction of pairs of spaces (X;,, M,,) that
    satisfy uniformly the inf-sup condition (1.12). The underlying idea due to Boland
    & Nicolaides [11], is that if (1.12) holds uniformly for a pair of spaces (Xp Mp)
    then one can generate a whole family of pairs of spaces that also satisfy (1.12)
    uniformly provided they satisfy a local inf-sup condition. In other words, the
    global condition (1.12) can be reduced to a local condition, which is of course
    much easier to check.
