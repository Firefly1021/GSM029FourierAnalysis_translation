# Sample terminology and names report

## Scope

The report covers PDF pages 37--51 (printed pages 25--39). Candidates were extracted from the structured sample and checked against rendered pages. No candidate has been marked `approved`.

## Terminology

- Nineteen terminology candidates were added to `glossary/terminology.tsv` with `needs-review` status.
- Ambiguous or workflow-blocking items were also added to `glossary/unresolved.tsv`.
- Personal-name elements remain in their source form; only ordinary mathematical nouns have proposed Chinese translations.

## Names

- Twenty-four source forms were added to `glossary/proper-names.tsv`.
- Accents in `Cesàro`, `Calderón`, and `de Guzmán` are retained.
- Initials, spacing, order, and capitalization in bibliographic prose are retained exactly as observed.
- No name was translated, transliterated, expanded, or reordered.

## Required review

All entries remain candidates. Human approval of terminology and a comparison of each name against the source image are required before the full-book workflow may begin.
