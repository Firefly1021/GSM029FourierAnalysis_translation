# Representative sample selection

- Selected PDF pages: **37–51** (15 continuous pages).
- Corresponding printed pages: **25–39**.
- Scope: Chapter 2, from its opening through the beginning of “Notes and further results.”

## Selection basis

This range is not the first 15 pages. It was selected after whole-book diagnosis because it contains a dense, continuous combination of ordinary prose and mathematical structures: chapter and section headings, definitions in prose, Theorems 2.1, 2.2, 2.4, 2.5, 2.10, 2.11, 2.15, and 2.16; Lemmas 2.6 and 2.12; Propositions 2.3, 2.7, and 2.14; Corollaries 2.8, 2.9, and 2.13; short and multi-page proofs; enumerated conclusions; inline mathematics; centered, multiline, and numbered display equations; cross-references to earlier equations, theorems, and chapters; and eponymous terms and names including Hardy, Littlewood, Marcinkiewicz, Calderón, Zygmund, Lebesgue, Wiener, and Riesz.

## Elements not covered

The selected continuous range contains no figure, formal table, explicit footnote, double-column page, appendix, bibliography page, or index page. The supplied PDF itself ends at printed page 216 and does not include the bibliography and index announced in its table of contents.

## Review status

The sample is representative for prose, theorem/proof structure, formulas, cross-references, and personal-name preservation. Because the PDF is a scan with hidden OCR, formula transcription and some OCR line segmentation require manual review.
