# Section 3.2 source observations

Scope: PDF pages 102-112 / printed pages 90-100. These are source observations, not corrections to the mathematical text. Original page images remain authoritative. None currently prevents reading the printed source.

1. PDF 102: the introduction explicitly imposes both complex symmetry and non-Hermitian character. Both conditions are retained; the translation does not replace this with a broader definition of complex symmetric matrices.
2. PDF 104, Algorithm 3.8: the inputs initialize v_0 and v_1; numbered lines 1 and 2 again use v_1, and line 1's numerator has no visible comma between its two factors. These printed forms are retained, not silently replaced by an inferred corrected algorithm.
3. PDF 105: the displayed span begins with v_1, although the algorithm input uses v_0. The printed indexing is retained.
4. PDF 107: the displayed identity after “From (3.41)” has (AR_n)^T AR_n on the left and R_n^T A R_(n+1) T_(n+1,n) on the right. No extra A is inserted into the right-hand side.
5. PDF 108: the prose defines d_k using the bilinear product of r_k with r_k, without the A present in the numerator of the following equation (3.51). Both are retained as printed.
6. PDF 110: the Givens paragraph prints Q_n^H Q_n = I_n, together with its rectangular projected-matrix equation. The subscript n is preserved rather than dimensionally “corrected”.
7. PDF 112: Algorithm 3.11 belongs to Section 3.2 although it floats beyond the Section 3.3 heading on PDF 111. The complete 28-line algorithm is included in this batch. Its line 13 reads “end for” in the original image; a draft transcription missing “end” was caught and corrected during visual QA before acceptance.
