\subsection{{Condition Number}}\label{sec:condition-number}
% block: perturbation-equation page:17 type:ordinary-paragraph
{Consider $A\bm x=\bm b$; if the matrix $A$ and right-hand side vector $\bm b$ are slightly changed, then by how much will the solution be changed? To answer this question, let $\Delta A$ and $\Delta\bm b$ be perturbations of $A$ and $\bm b$. Then the change of the solution denoted by $\Delta\bm x$ satisfies the following relation:}
\[
(A+\Delta A)(\bm x+\Delta\bm x)=\bm b+\Delta\bm b.
\]
{Using $A\bm x=\bm b$ gives}
\[
\Delta\bm x=A^{-1}[-\Delta A(\bm x+\Delta\bm x)+\Delta\bm b].
\]
{Using Theorem~\ref{thm:matrix-norm-properties}-(\ref{part:matrix-norm-action}) and the triangle inequality (\ref{cond:vector-norm-triangle}), the $p$-norm of $\bm x$ can be evaluated by}
\[
\|\Delta\bm x\|_p\le\|A^{-1}\|_p(\|\Delta A\|_p\|\bm x+\Delta\bm x\|_p+\|\Delta\bm b\|_p).
\]
{Thus, we have}
\begin{equation}\label{eq:relative-perturbation-bound}
\frac{\|\Delta\bm x\|_p}{\|\bm x+\Delta\bm x\|_p}
\le\|A\|_p\|A^{-1}\|_p
\left(\frac{\|\Delta A\|_p}{\|A\|_p}+
\frac{\|\Delta\bm b\|_p}{\|A\|_p\|\bm x+\Delta\bm x\|_p}\right).
\end{equation}
% block: condition-number-definition page:17 type:ordinary-paragraph
{Here,}
\begin{equation}\label{eq:p-norm-condition-number}
\kappa_p(A):=\|A\|_p\|A^{-1}\|_p
\end{equation}
{is called the $p$-norm condition number. Inequality \eqref{eq:relative-perturbation-bound} implies that if the condition number is large, the computed approximate solution may be far from the exact solution. Note that this fact does not depend on what algorithms are used for solving linear systems.}
% block: ill-conditioning page:17 type:ordinary-paragraph
{If the condition number is large, then the linear systems are called ill-conditioned. We will see in Chapter~\ref{chap:krylov-classification} that the condition number also affects the speed of convergence of Krylov subspace methods.}
{In this book, we often use a 2-norm condition number $\kappa_2(A)$ that will be simply denoted by $\kappa$ or $\operatorname{cond}(A)$.}
