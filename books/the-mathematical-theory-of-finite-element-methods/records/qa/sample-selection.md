# Representative sample selection

The controller first selected PDF pages 2, 1 and 171 as the first native, scanned and mixed pages. Those choices are retained in `qa/sample-plan.json` as mechanical classification evidence.

The translated natural unit is PDF pages 171-173, complete Section 6.3, `The Multigrid Algorithm`. It begins at the section heading on page 171 and stops immediately before Section 6.4 on page 173.

This range was selected because it contains:

- ordinary mathematical prose;
- a four-panel figure and caption;
- a definition crossing a page boundary;
- numbered and unnumbered formulas;
- a recursive algorithm;
- an internal figure reference;
- references to objects outside the sample;
- multigrid terminology and notation.

The scanned cover and series page were inspected for source type and exact personal names but were not treated as translated Chinese body text.
