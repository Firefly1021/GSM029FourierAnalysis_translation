若系数足够光滑, 可以将一般 PDE 写成散度形式. 这里给出实现这一转换的充分条件, 但这些条件绝非必要条件.

\begin{Lemma}
\label{lem:smooth-coefficients-divergence-conversion}
设
\begin{equation}\label{eq:divergence-conversion-higher-coefficient-smoothness}
a_\alpha\in C_b^{|\alpha|-k}(\overline\Omega)\quad\text{for }k<|\alpha|\leq2k
\end{equation}
且
\begin{equation}\label{eq:divergence-conversion-lower-coefficient-continuity}
a_\alpha\in C_b(\overline\Omega)\quad\text{for }|\alpha|\leq k.
\end{equation}
那么存在 $a_{\sigma\gamma}\in C_b^{|\sigma|}(\overline\Omega)$, 使得对任意 $u\in C^{2k}(\Omega)$, 有\[\begin{aligned}
L(\bm{x},D)u&=\sum_{|\alpha|\leq2k}a_\alpha(\bm{x})D^\alpha\\
&=\sum_{0\leq|\sigma|,|\gamma|\leq k}(-1)^{|\sigma|}D^\sigma(a_{\sigma\gamma}(\bm{x})D^\gamma u).
\end{aligned}\]
\end{Lemma}

\begin{Proof}
这里对一般的 $k$ 给出证明, 但记号相当繁复, 因此读者应当在 $k=1$ 的情形下逐一验证细节.

对每个 $|\alpha|\leq2k$, 选取 $\sigma_\alpha$ 和 $\gamma_\alpha$, 满足 $|\sigma_\alpha|,|\gamma_\alpha|\leq k$, $\sigma_\alpha+\gamma_\alpha=\alpha$. 当然, 这种选取并不唯一.

现在对任意 $u\in C^{2k}(\Omega)$ 和 $\phi\in\mathcal{D}(\Omega)$, 有\[\begin{aligned}
\int_\Omega L(\bm{x},D)u\,\phi\,\mathrm{d}\bm{x}
&=\sum_{|\alpha|\leq2k}\int_\Omega(D^\alpha u)a_\alpha\phi\,\mathrm{d}\bm{x}\\
&=\sum_{|\alpha|\leq2k}\int_\Omega(D^{\sigma_\alpha+\gamma_\alpha}u)a_{(\sigma_\alpha+\gamma_\alpha)}\phi\,\mathrm{d}\bm{x}\\
&=\sum_{|\alpha|\leq2k}(-1)^{|\sigma_\alpha|}\int_\Omega(D^{\gamma_\alpha}u)\bigl(D^{\sigma_\alpha}[a_{(\sigma_\alpha+\gamma_\alpha)}\phi]\bigr)\,\mathrm{d}\bm{x}\\
&=\sum_{|\alpha|\leq2k}(-1)^{|\sigma_\alpha|}\int_\Omega D^{\gamma_\alpha}u
\sum_{\rho\leq\sigma_\alpha}\binom{\sigma_\alpha}{\rho}D^{\sigma_\alpha-\rho}a_{(\sigma_\alpha+\gamma_\alpha)}D^\rho\phi\,\mathrm{d}\bm{x}\\
&=\sum_{\substack{|\alpha|\leq2k\\\rho\leq\sigma_\alpha}}(-1)^{|\sigma_\alpha|+|\rho|}\int_\Omega
D^\rho\left[\binom{\sigma_\alpha}{\rho}D^{\sigma_\alpha-\rho}a_{(\sigma_\alpha+\gamma_\alpha)}D^{\gamma_\alpha}u\right]\phi\,\mathrm{d}\bm{x}\\
&=: \sum_{0\leq|\sigma|,|\gamma|\leq k}\int_\Omega(-1)^{|\sigma|}D^\sigma(a_{\sigma\gamma}(\bm{x})D^\gamma u)\phi\,\mathrm{d}\bm{x}.
\end{aligned}\](注意, 最后一个等式是一个定义.) 由于这对所有 $\phi\in\mathcal{D}(\Omega)$ 成立, 所需结论得证.
\end{Proof}

\begin{Remark}
\label{rem:standing-elliptic-coefficient-smoothness}
除非另有明确说明, 均假设系数满足光滑性假设 \eqref{eq:divergence-conversion-higher-coefficient-smoothness} 和 \eqref{eq:divergence-conversion-lower-coefficient-continuity}.
\end{Remark}
