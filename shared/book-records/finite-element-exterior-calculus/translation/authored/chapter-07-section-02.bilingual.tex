\subsection{\BT{The Koszul complex}{Koszul 复形}}\label{sec:koszul-complex}

% block: koszul-identity-vector-field page:93 type:ordinary-paragraph
\BT{For $x\in\Omega\subset\mathbb R^n$, the tangent space $T_x\Omega$ may be identified with $\mathbb R^n$, so we obtain a vector field by assigning to each point $x\in\Omega$ that same $x$, now viewed as a vector in its tangent space. This identity vector field is display in Figure~\ref{fig:identity-vector-field}.}{对 $x\in\Omega\subset\mathbb R^n$, 可将切空间 $T_x\Omega$ 与 $\mathbb R^n$ 等同, 因而为每个点 $x\in\Omega$ 指定同一个 $x$, 但将其看作切空间中的向量, 就得到一个向量场. 此恒等向量场见 Figure~\ref{fig:identity-vector-field}.}

% block: koszul-differential-definition page:93 type:ordinary-paragraph
\BT{The \emph{Koszul differential} $\kappa\omega=\omega\lrcorner\operatorname{id}$ of a $k$-form $\omega$ is the $(k-1)$-form obtained by contracting $\omega$ with the identity vector field. That is,}{$k$-形式 $\omega$ 的 \emph{Koszul 微分} $\kappa\omega=\omega\lrcorner\operatorname{id}$, 是将 $\omega$ 与恒等向量场缩并得到的 $(k-1)$-形式. 即}
\[
\begin{gathered}
(\kappa\omega)_x(v_1,\ldots,v_{k-1})=\omega_x(x,v_1,\ldots,v_{k-1}),\\
x\in\Omega,\ v_1,\ldots,v_{k-1}\in\mathbb R^n,
\end{gathered}
\]
\BT{where inside the parentheses on the right-hand side the point $x$ is viewed as a tangent vector. On $0$-forms, $\kappa$ is understood to be zero. Since $\omega(x,x,\ldots)\equiv0$, $\kappa\circ\kappa=0$, so $\kappa$ is indeed a differential. Note that}{其中右端括号内的点 $x$ 被看作切向量. 在 $0$-形式上, 规定 $\kappa$ 为零. 由于 $\omega(x,x,\ldots)\equiv0$, 有 $\kappa\circ\kappa=0$, 所以 $\kappa$ 确实是微分. 注意}
\[
\kappa(dx^i)=x^i,
\]
\BT{i.e., the Koszul differential of the basic $1$-form $dx^i$ is the $i$th coordinate function. Since $\kappa$ is defined by contraction with a vector field, it satisfies a Leibniz rule:}{即基本 $1$-形式 $dx^i$ 的 Koszul 微分是第 $i$ 个坐标函数. 由于 $\kappa$ 通过与向量场缩并来定义, 它满足 Leibniz 法则:}
\[
\kappa(\omega\wedge\mu)=(\kappa\omega)\wedge\mu+(-1)^k\omega\wedge(\kappa\mu).
\]
\BT{In particular, $\kappa(f\omega)=f\kappa\omega$ for any differential form $\omega$ and any function $f$, so, for example, $\kappa(f\,dx^i)=f\,x^i$. These rules determine $\kappa$ completely. For example,}{特别地, 对任意微分形式 $\omega$ 和任意函数 $f$, 有 $\kappa(f\omega)=f\kappa\omega$, 因而例如 $\kappa(f\,dx^i)=f\,x^i$. 这些法则完全确定了 $\kappa$. 例如,}
\[
\begin{aligned}
\kappa(dx^i\wedge dx^j)&=x^i\,dx^j-x^j\,dx^i,\\
\kappa(dx^i\wedge dx^j\wedge dx^k)&=x^i\,dx^j\wedge dx^k-x^j\,dx^i\wedge dx^k+x^k\,dx^i\wedge dx^j.
\end{aligned}
\]

\begin{figure}[htbp]\centering

\caption{\BT{The identity vector field.}{恒等向量场.}}\label{fig:identity-vector-field}

% block: koszul-vector-proxies page:94 type:ordinary-paragraph
\BT{If we identify $1$-forms with vector fields, then $\kappa$ corresponds to the dot product of the vector field with $x$ (or, more properly, with the identity vector field). Applied to $2$-forms in three dimensions, $\kappa$ is the cross product with $x$, and on $3$-forms it is the product of a scalar field with $x$ to get a vector field.}{若将 $1$-形式与向量场等同, 则 $\kappa$ 对应于向量场与 $x$ 的点积 (更准确地说, 是与恒等向量场的点积). 应用于三维中的 $2$-形式时, $\kappa$ 是与 $x$ 的叉积; 在 $3$-形式上, 它是标量场与 $x$ 相乘而得到向量场.}

% block: koszul-homotopy-introduction page:94 type:ordinary-paragraph
\BT{The Koszul differential $\kappa$ maps $\mathcal P_r\Lambda^k$ to $\mathcal P_{r+1}\Lambda^{k-1}$, increasing the polynomial degree and decreasing the form degree, exactly the reverse of $d$. Thus both $\kappa d$ and $d\kappa$ map $\mathcal P_r\Lambda^k$ to itself. The following theorem points to an intimate relation between $\kappa$ and $d$, called the \emph{homotopy formula}. In it, we write $\mathcal H_r\Lambda^k$ for the $k$-forms with \emph{homogeneous} polynomial coefficients of degree $r$.}{Koszul 微分 $\kappa$ 将 $\mathcal P_r\Lambda^k$ 映到 $\mathcal P_{r+1}\Lambda^{k-1}$, 提高多项式次数并降低形式次数, 与 $d$ 恰好相反. 因此 $\kappa d$ 和 $d\kappa$ 都将 $\mathcal P_r\Lambda^k$ 映到自身. 下述定理揭示了 $\kappa$ 与 $d$ 之间的密切关系, 称为\emph{同伦公式}. 其中用 $\mathcal H_r\Lambda^k$ 表示系数为次数 $r$ 的\emph{齐次}多项式的 $k$-形式.}

% block: koszul-homotopy-theorem page:94 type:theorem
\Needspace{6\baselineskip}
\begin{Theorem}\label{thm:koszul-homotopy}
\[
(d\kappa+\kappa d)\omega=(k+r)\omega,\qquad\omega\in\mathcal H_r\Lambda^k.
\]
\end{Theorem}

% block: koszul-homotopy-proof page:94 type:proof
\begin{Proof}
\BT{We prove this by induction on $k$, the case $k=0$ being Euler's identity $x\cdot\operatorname{grad}p=r\,p$ for $p$ a homogeneous polynomial of degree $r$. Thus it is enough to prove the result for the $(k+1)$-form $dx^i\wedge\omega$ assuming that it is true for $\omega\in\mathcal H_r\Lambda^k$. Using the Leibniz rules for $\kappa$ and $d$ and the identity $d^2=0$, we have}{对 $k$ 归纳证明; 情形 $k=0$ 就是次数为 $r$ 的齐次多项式 $p$ 满足的 Euler 恒等式 $x\cdot\operatorname{grad}p=r\,p$. 因此, 假设结论对 $\omega\in\mathcal H_r\Lambda^k$ 成立, 只需证明它对 $(k+1)$-形式 $dx^i\wedge\omega$ 成立. 使用 $\kappa$ 和 $d$ 的 Leibniz 法则以及恒等式 $d^2=0$, 有}
\[
\begin{aligned}
d\kappa(dx^i\wedge\omega)&=d(x^i\omega-dx^i\wedge\kappa\omega)\\
&=dx^i\wedge\omega+x^i d\omega+dx^i\wedge d\kappa\omega
\end{aligned}
\]
\BT{and}{以及}
\[
\kappa d(dx^i\wedge\omega)=-\kappa(dx^i\wedge d\omega)=-x^i d\omega+dx^i\wedge\kappa d\omega.
\]
\BT{Therefore}{因此}
\[
\begin{aligned}
(d\kappa+\kappa d)(dx^i\wedge\omega)&=dx^i\wedge\omega+dx^i\wedge(d\kappa+\kappa d)\omega\\
&=dx^i\wedge\omega+(k+r)dx^i\wedge\omega\\
&=(k+r+1)dx^i\wedge\omega,
\end{aligned}
\]
\BT{where we have invoked the inductive hypothesis to obtain the second equality. This completes the proof.}{其中使用归纳假设得到了第二个等式. 证明完成.}
\end{Proof}

% block: koszul-cartan-remark page:95 type:remark
\Needspace{6\baselineskip}
\begin{Remark}\label{rem:koszul-cartan-proof}
\BT{An alternative proof is based on Cartan's \emph{homotopy formula} of differential geometry. See Theorem 3.1 of \BookCite{11}.}{另一个证明基于微分几何中 Cartan 的\emph{同伦公式}. 见 \BookCite{11} 的 Theorem 3.1.}
\end{Remark}

% block: polynomial-de-rham-exactness page:95 type:corollary
\Needspace{8\baselineskip}
\begin{Corollary}\label{cor:polynomial-de-rham-exactness}
\BT{The polynomial de Rham complex}{多项式 de Rham 复形}
\[
\mathcal P_r\Lambda^0\xrightarrow{d}\mathcal P_{r-1}\Lambda^1\xrightarrow{d}\cdots\xrightarrow{d}\mathcal P_{r-n}\Lambda^n\to0
\]
\BT{and the Koszul complex}{和 Koszul 复形}
\[
0\to\mathcal P_{r-n}\Lambda^n\xrightarrow{\kappa}\mathcal P_{r-n+1}\Lambda^{n-1}\xrightarrow{\kappa}\cdots\xrightarrow{\kappa}\mathcal P_r\Lambda^0
\]
\BT{are both exact.}{都是正合的.}
\end{Corollary}

% block: polynomial-exactness-endpoints page:95 type:ordinary-paragraph
\BT{Note that we have left the $0$ off from one end of each complex, since we would not have exactness there.}{注意, 在每个复形的一端省略了 $0$, 因为在那里不具有正合性.}

% block: polynomial-exactness-proof page:95 type:proof
\begin{Proof}
\BT{We establish this for the homogeneous polynomial de Rham complex}{先对齐次多项式 de Rham 复形证明此结论:}
\[
\mathcal H_r\Lambda^0\xrightarrow{d}\mathcal H_{r-1}\Lambda^1\xrightarrow{d}\cdots\xrightarrow{d}\mathcal H_{r-n}\Lambda^n\to0.
\]
\BT{We can then just sum to get the result. We must show that if $\omega\in\mathcal H_s\Lambda^k$ and $d\omega=0$, then $\omega$ is in the range of $d$. Indeed,}{随后求和即可得到结果. 需要证明, 若 $\omega\in\mathcal H_s\Lambda^k$ 且 $d\omega=0$, 则 $\omega$ 属于 $d$ 的像. 事实上,}
\[
\omega=(s+k)^{-1}(d\kappa+\kappa d)\omega=(s+k)^{-1}d\kappa\omega.
\]
\BT{A similar proof holds for the Koszul complex.}{Koszul 复形也有类似证明.}
\end{Proof}

% block: homogeneous-direct-sum-introduction page:95 type:ordinary-paragraph
\BT{Another important consequence is a direct sum decomposition.}{另一个重要推论是直和分解.}

% block: homogeneous-direct-sum page:95 type:corollary
\Needspace{6\baselineskip}
\begin{Corollary}\label{cor:homogeneous-koszul-decomposition}
\begin{equation}\label{eq:homogeneous-koszul-decomposition}
\mathcal H_r\Lambda^k=\kappa\mathcal H_{r-1}\Lambda^{k+1}\oplus d\mathcal H_{r+1}\Lambda^{k-1}.
\end{equation}
\end{Corollary}

% block: homogeneous-direct-sum-proof page:95 type:proof
\begin{Proof}
\BT{By the homotopy formula, any element of $\mathcal H_r\Lambda^k$ belongs to $\kappa\mathcal H_{r-1}\Lambda^{k+1}+d\mathcal H_{r+1}\Lambda^{k-1}$. Moreover the intersection of these two spaces is zero, since if $\omega$ belongs to the intersection, then $d\omega=0$, $\kappa\omega=0$, so $\omega=0$ by the homotopy formula.}{由同伦公式, $\mathcal H_r\Lambda^k$ 的任意元素都属于 $\kappa\mathcal H_{r-1}\Lambda^{k+1}+d\mathcal H_{r+1}\Lambda^{k-1}$. 此外, 两个空间的交为零, 因为若 $\omega$ 属于交集, 则 $d\omega=0$, $\kappa\omega=0$, 从而由同伦公式得到 $\omega=0$.}
\end{Proof}
