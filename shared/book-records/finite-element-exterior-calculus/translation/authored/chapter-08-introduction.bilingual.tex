\section{\BT{Further directions and applications}{进一步方向与应用}}\label{chap:further-directions}

% block: further-directions-hodge-formulation page:107 type:ordinary-paragraph
\BT{So far we have considered as our Hilbert complex the $L^2$ de Rham complex on a domain in $\mathbb R^n$, so that the resulting operator is the Hodge Laplacian. For $0\leq k\leq n$, the segment \eqref{eq:finite-element-forms-short-complex} gives rise to the weak formulation: given $f\in L^2\Lambda^k$, find $\sigma\in H\Lambda^{k-1}$, $u\in H\Lambda^k$, and $p\in\mathsf H^k$ such that}{迄今, 我们所考虑的 Hilbert 复形是 $\mathbb R^n$ 中区域上的 $L^2$ de Rham 复形, 因而得到的算子是 Hodge Laplacian. 对 $0\leq k\leq n$, 片段 \eqref{eq:finite-element-forms-short-complex} 导出弱形式: 给定 $f\in L^2\Lambda^k$, 求 $\sigma\in H\Lambda^{k-1}$, $u\in H\Lambda^k$ 和 $p\in\mathsf H^k$, 使得}
\begin{equation}\label{eq:general-hodge-weak-formulation}
\begin{aligned}
\langle\sigma,\tau\rangle-\langle u,d\tau\rangle&=0,&\tau&\in H\Lambda^{k-1},\\
\langle d\sigma,v\rangle+\langle du,dv\rangle+\langle p,v\rangle&=\langle f,v\rangle,&v&\in H\Lambda^k,\\
\langle u,q\rangle&=0,&q&\in\mathsf H^k,
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
\BT{for which the corresponding strong formulation is given in \eqref{eq:finite-element-forms-hodge-strong}. We have shown stability, consistency, and convergence for the Galerkin solution to this problem using any of the four pairs of spaces in \eqref{eq:four-galerkin-form-pairs} and any polynomial degree $r\geq1$. The corresponding rates of convergence, for smooth solutions, are shown in Table~\ref{tab:galerkin-form-approximation-rates}.}{相应的强形式见 \eqref{eq:finite-element-forms-hodge-strong}. 已经证明, 使用 \eqref{eq:four-galerkin-form-pairs} 中任意一对空间及任意多项式次数 $r\geq1$ 时, 此问题的 Galerkin 解具有稳定性, 相容性和收敛性. 对光滑解, 相应收敛阶见 Table~\ref{tab:galerkin-form-approximation-rates}.}
